About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Notre Dame Department of Mathematics, working with Mark Behrens.

I completed my Ph.D. in Spring 2025 at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Agnès Beaudry.

Before that, I was an undergraduate at the University of Texas at Austin, where I received Bachelors of Science in math and physics.

My research interests lie generally in the areas of stable homotopy theory, and interactions between algebraic topology and physics.

Research

Ongoing

  1. The Real v_n-Bockstein spectral sequence.
    The pictures at the top and bottom of this page illustrate a Bockstein spectral sequence computing the homotopy groups of tmf_0(3) from the RO(C_2)-graded homotopy groups of Atiyah Real K-theory. I'm currently thinking about generalizations of this computation to truncated norms of Real bordism.
  2. Telescopes and the F_p-synthetic ANSS
    With Mark Behrens, we study localizations of the F_p-synthetic ANSS of certain synthetic spectra. Of particular interest to us are the synthetic analogs of the Mahowald--Ravenel--Schick Thom spectra y(n).
