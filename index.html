About Me Research Teaching

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Notre Dame working with Mark Behrens. I completed my Ph.D. in Spring 2025 at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Agnès Beaudry. Before that, I was an undergraduate at the University of Texas at Austin, where I received Bachelors of Science in math and physics.

My research interests lie generally in the areas of stable homotopy theory, and interactions between algebraic topology and physics. You can find my CV here (last updated Sep. 2026).
